================================================================================
                    LOCATIONCONTROL - HOW TO RUN
================================================================================

PROJECT FOLDER:
C:\Users\Creep\Downloads\PROJECTS\locationcontrol


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 1: THE EASIEST WAY (1-CLICK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to:
   C:\Users\Creep\Downloads\PROJECTS\locationcontrol

2. Double-click:
   START_SERVER.bat

   That's it! A black window will open and run the companion server.
   Leave that window open while using the app.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 2: USING POWERSHELL / COMMAND PROMPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open PowerShell or Command Prompt.

2. Run:
   python -m locationctl serve
   (or simply: locationctl serve)

3. You will see:
   ======== Running on http://0.0.0.0:8765 ========


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE FROM YOUR IPHONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect your iPhone 13 to your PC using the USB cable.
   Unlock your iPhone screen (tap "Trust" if it asks).

2. Make sure your iPhone is on your home Wi-Fi.

3. Open the "LocationControl" app on your iPhone.

4. In the app settings / companion setup, make sure the URL is:
   http://192.168.1.93:8765

5. On the map:
   - Tap anywhere in the world to drop a pin (or search for an address).
   - Tap the blue "SPOOF LOCATION" button.
   - The badge will turn GREEN ("SPOOFING ACTIVE").
   - Open Apple Maps / Google Maps on your phone to confirm your new location!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO KEEP THE SPOOF ACTIVE AFTER UNPLUGGING & LEAVING THE HOUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. While plugged in, spoof your desired location (status is green).
2. DO NOT press STOP in the app.
3. DO NOT stop the PC server.
4. Simply unplug the USB cable from your iPhone!
5. iOS will freeze that fake location in memory.
6. Important: Turn off Wi-Fi on your iPhone when outside (in iPhone Settings > Wi-Fi)
   so apps don't triangulate nearby router signals.
7. To restore real GPS when you come back:
   Either plug back in and tap STOP, or just restart your iPhone!
