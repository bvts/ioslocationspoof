================================================================================
                    LOCATIONCONTROL - HOW TO RUN & TROUBLESHOOT
================================================================================

PROJECT DIRECTORY:
C:\Users\Creep\Downloads\PROJECTS\locationcontrol


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STARTING THE COMPANION SERVER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A (Easiest - 1 Click):
  Simply double-click:
    START_SERVER.bat
  A window will open and display:
    [+] Web Map UI:           http://localhost:8765
    [+] iPhone Companion API: http://<YOUR_LOCAL_IP>:8765

Option B (Command Line):
  Open PowerShell or Command Prompt in this folder and run:
    python -m locationctl serve
    (or: locationctl serve)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONNECTING & SPOOFING FROM YOUR IPHONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect your iPhone to your PC with the USB cable.
   - Unlock your iPhone screen.
   - Tap "Trust This Computer" if prompted and enter your iPhone passcode.

2. Open the "LocationControl" app on your iPhone.
   - If prompted for Companion API URL, enter the IP shown in the server window:
     Example: http://192.168.1.93:8765
   - Tap "Test Connection" to confirm it connects.

3. On the interactive map:
   - Tap anywhere to drop a pin, or type a landmark/city in the search bar.
   - Tap the blue button: "SPOOF LOCATION".
   - The badge turns GREEN ("• SPOOFING ACTIVE").
   - Check Google Maps, Apple Maps, Tinder, Uber, etc. on your phone—your GPS is changed!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEEPING THE SPOOF ACTIVE AFTER LEAVING YOUR HOUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. While plugged into your PC, spoof your desired coordinates (badge is green).
2. DO NOT tap "STOP" in the app.
3. Simply unplug the USB cable from your iPhone!
4. Apple's iOS location service (locationd) freezes that simulated coordinate in memory.
5. Important when outside: Turn OFF Wi-Fi in iPhone Settings > Wi-Fi (so apps
   cannot cross-reference nearby router BSSID addresses).
6. To restore your real GPS when you return:
   Either plug back into the PC and tap STOP, or simply RESTART your iPhone
   (Hold Volume Down + Power -> slide to power off).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHY DID THE APP STOP OPENING AFTER 7 DAYS? (SIDELOADLY RE-SIGNING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you installed the app using Sideloadly with a free Apple ID:
- Apple strictly enforces a 7-day expiration on free developer certificates.
- After 7 days, iOS will not launch the app ("App No Longer Available").
- FIX (Takes 30 seconds):
  1. Open Sideloadly on your PC.
  2. Plug your iPhone in via USB.
  3. Drag LocationControl.ipa into Sideloadly.
  4. Click "Start" to re-sign and install. Your settings will be preserved!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MAP CONFIGURATION & API KEYS (.env)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By default, the server uses free, high-contrast dark CartoDB tiles and server-proxied
OpenStreetMap geocoding with no API key required.

If you wish to use a custom provider (Geoapify, Mapbox, Stadia Maps):
1. Copy .env.example to .env
2. Set your provider and key:
     MAP_PROVIDER=geoapify
     MAP_API_KEY=your_key_here
3. Restart the server. The Web Map UI will automatically use your custom provider!
